About the Diocese

A local Church centered in Dumaguete.

The Diocese of Dumaguete was established on April 5, 1955 and is a suffragan diocese of the Archdiocese of Cebu. Its cathedral is the St. Catherine of Alexandria Cathedral Parish in Dumaguete City.

The diocese is shepherded by Most Rev. Julito B. Cortes, D.D., Bishop of Dumaguete. This portal focuses on public-facing sacramental record services and certificate requests.
